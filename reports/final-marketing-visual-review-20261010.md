# Final marketing and document visual review

10 October 2026. Independent read-only review after the final 23-route follow-up merged into the main matrix.

## Coverage and result

Actually viewed all 36 content routes at 375px WebKit and 1280px Chrome: 72 fresh route/width captures, with header, middle body and footer crops (216 regions). All 18 final contact sheets were opened and visually reviewed. This includes 35 public marketing/document routes and the unauthenticated admin login gate. Authenticated dashboard content is outside this result.

Captures stay outside Git under the existing private review output: `output/playwright/craft-final/`. Reviewed sheets are `content-review-crops/375-1.jpg` through `375-9.jpg` and `1280-1.jpg` through `1280-9.jpg`. These are derived from the final captures, not reused from an earlier pass. Alpha-transparent capture edges were composited onto the brand paper colour.

No remaining visible clipping, overlapping navigation, duplicate footer or broken layout found in these reviewed regions. Shared paper/burgundy identity and typography align across the public wrapper pages. The distinct sample artwork remains distinct. Wider blog openings resolve the earlier excessive desktop headline wrapping while keeping body text narrow. Receptionist sample playback remains a useful foreground control within its contextual hero. Home now shows actual restaurant concept artwork and the contextual phone composition with readable labels.

## Flags resolved with direct evidence

- Mobile onboarding black gutter was a capture artifact. Normal WebKit viewport PNG is375x900 with opaque paper at the right edge. Browser innerWidth/clientWidth/body/document/scrollWidth are375 and scrollbar width0. The full-page PNG was396x5084 with a21px alpha-zero edge; converting directly toRGB rendered it black. No page CSS change was required.
- The initially blank desktop Dental Practice preview was a lazy-loading capture artifact. Independent actual scrolling loaded the existing image at naturalWidth960, opacity1 and complete=true. The actual image screenshot was viewed. The final merged gallery screenshot also displays the image.
- Three article headings now use a wider opening area. Final mobile and desktop heading crops were viewed; no clipped words found.

## Claims check

Confirmed the final source removes the unsupported dental pilot34-to58percent claim, restaurant50percent no-show promise and Motor Factors80percent account-recognition claim. Dental recall, restaurant deposits/SMS and motor-factor account recognition now depend on the configured plan, booking or integration. The missed-calls article social description now uses the customer's own records and stated assumptions. Its visible calculation is explicitly illustrative, not a customer result or universal statistic. Prices, coverage hours, included minutes, setup fees and legal commitments were preserved. CSS percentages and example trade discounts were not treated as performance claims.

This visual review is not a legal rewrite or an independent verification of every factual term. It does not claim provider actions, form submission, payment, authenticated admin testing, production publication or a true 3D implementation.
