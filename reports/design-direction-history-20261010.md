# Recovered CallMeIE design requirements

10 October 2026. This is the distilled design contract from original user
messages. The private exact-quote ledger and database provenance remain in
Codex durable memory outside this public website repository. Recovery included
the existing ask_system.py routing and Codex rollout database, direct Claude
and Codex user-history stores, maintained design documents, and source messages.
Role-merged or truncated database text was not treated as an original user quote.
This was a systematic relevant-message search, not a claim that every byte of
all raw session files was read.

## Explicit requirements to carry into the next composition

1. **Preserve the established CallMeIE character.** Adam names the Great National pilot proposal pack as the house-theme reference on10October. Its paper/ink/burgundy and strong typography establish identity, but applying those colours and fonts alone is insufficient. Do not revert to an unrelated generic dark agency theme or a plain document layout.
2. **Build an art-directed whole page with multiple custom assets.** The10October ask explicitly names premium, ultra-HD professional imagery, custom layouts/structure/elements and multiple generated assets. Integrate imagery, text, lighting, texture, framing and controls into a composition. The floating standalone phone is rejected. April directions already demanded big imagery, changing visual scenes and more than fonts/colours.
3. **Research and actually see current high-craft sites before choosing the design.** Study best human work, inspect actual scrolling and intermediate frames, and convert findings into reusable skills. Do not claim that installing a library or copying a palette reaches the benchmark. Clancy Strand is the stated minimum, not the ceiling. Present-day2026 references must be visually inspected and checked against current primary sources; historical research is a starting point.
4. **Use generated visual studies to choose the composition.** Give imagegen the business brief and strong reference imagery with artistic freedom; request several genuinely different visual alternatives. Do not over-constrain it to the agent's first idea. Plan useful text space, camera/framing, dimensions, destination, layers and motion states. Use generated assets where they materially improve the page, including texture, frames, outlines or borders. October history4270 explicitly rejects an upgrade consisting only of tilted boxes;4296 asks for the model, space, lighting, texture packs and node-based workflows;4317 selects a scene and asks for the other objects, text, frames, timing, lighting, shadows, depth and effects to be designed together. A generated asset is not a complete design by itself.
5. **Maintain concise, useful copy.** No emoji or repeated decorative arrow glyphs. No staccato slogan pairs, full-stop/new-line narration or a differently coloured final word. Avoid AI-style internal process narration, invented proof and unnecessary numbers. Explain the offer, what the customer sees, why it helps, price/terms and next action. Fewer words must not mean less visual craft.
6. **Give every small control proper design attention.** The10October sample voice player must be a custom, polished playback surface rather than a default-looking little play bar. Forms, gallery navigation, buttons, frames and labels are part of the composition. Consolidate overlapping floating controls and banners. No implementation detail should compete with a customer's task.
7. **Make motion purposeful, smoothly reversible and readable.** April24 requests independent custom image layers and movement tied frame by frame to scroll. Page and object must move fluidly together. Reject looping in place, pinning the page unnecessarily, image swapping/glitches and reverse-scroll snap resets. Text must remain readable and travel with a continuous scene when that is the selected direction. Do not require excessive physical scrolling just to make animation smoother.
8. **Compose mobile separately.** Mobile cannot be a squeezed desktop crop. Header, CTA, scene, text, menu, footer and forms must fit320/375/390 and tablet widths. No sideways page movement, clipped text, hidden hero images or unreadable controls. Test real browser interactions and recordings in both directions; a scrollWidth gate does not prove visual quality.
9. **Keep parent-brand cohesion while preserving genuinely different demos.** Earlier explicit rejections target ten demos with the same flow, same cards, same rounded controls and same placements recoloured. Distinct demo art direction is required, including within the same industry. April24 accepted clean business demos as the baseline and separated graphic/SaaS/creative examples into another avenue. That does not authorise random corporate-page themes or downgrading demo quality.
10. **Demonstrate the service before demanding work.** Show a useful website preview and an honest receptionist call sample. Do not call a sales/intake conversation merely a demo. Keep pre-commitment selection light; gather detailed bespoke FAQs later. Gallery previews must render actual thumbnails, open actual sites and offer clear return/next/previous routes. Every visible link and control must work.
11. **Keep commercial truth current.** Do not carry historical Doc Ops or obsolete prices into current public copy. No invented customers/testimonials. Optional integrations must be distinct from the base offer. The current live/source commercial authority, seller notices and scope documents govern pricing and obligations; history explains preferences, not today's offer.
12. **Reuse and improve the established method.** Research → extract useful patterns → brief/PDR → prototype one → visually review → refine → batch. Reuse working assets, code, workflows and installed skills with the current business brief. Do not start generic CSS from scratch or freeze one successful template forever. Scope and working behaviour must survive design upgrades.
