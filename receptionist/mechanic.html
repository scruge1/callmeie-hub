§ 02 — Capabilities

What it handles for a garage

Six receptionist jobs, done by one system, tuned for Irish garage patterns — NCT pre-checks, services, urgent breakdowns, pre-purchase inspections, customer readiness updates, parts arrival.

I — NCT pre-check

NCT prep + booking

Caller mentions NCT — the AI books the right slot (90 minutes), quotes the price, asks the right prep questions (last service date, any warning lights, recent mileage). Sends a prep checklist by SMS so the customer arrives with the car ready.

II — Service

Full / interim service intake

Knows your service-pricing menu — full, interim, oil-change-only — and captures the rego, year, fuel, and any specific concerns ("noise from the front when braking," "warning light on yesterday"). The mechanic walks in already briefed.

III — Breakdown

Urgent + towing

"Car won't start," "I'm on the side of the road" — different flow. The AI captures location (Eircode + nearest landmark), the symptoms, and either pages you (if you handle towing) or refers to your towing partner with the customer's details forwarded.

IV — Pre-purchase

Pre-purchase inspection

Caller looking at a used car needs a third-party inspection. The AI captures the rego of the car they're considering, location of the inspection (caller's home, the dealer's, your garage), and books the slot. Quotes the price upfront.

V — Ready calls

"Is my car ready?"

The most-frequent call after bookings. The AI checks the workshop diary status — "in progress, expected back at 4," "waiting on a part, tomorrow morning," "ready now" — and answers honestly. Saves you stopping mid-job to take the call.

VI — Weekly report

Monday morning summary

Plain email Monday: bookings taken, services done, NCT pre-checks, breakdown calls, quote requests outstanding, missed calls (none, on Growth). Read in ninety seconds — no dashboard.

§ 03 — Depth

Built for Irish garages specifically

The stuff a generic "global" receptionist gets wrong every time: NCT vs MOT, RSA reg-plate parsing, the Irish service-vs-quote distinction, the "I want to know what's wrong with it before I bring it in" Irish customer pattern.

01Reg

Irish reg plates + RSA lookup

Knows both old (06-L-1234) and new (181-L-2345) Irish reg formats. Resolves to make, model, year, fuel, engine size via RSA register. Catches when a customer reads the year wrong ("she's a 2020" but the reg says 191 — "that's 2019, just to confirm"). Saves the mid-service surprise that the wrong parts were ordered.

Caller
"Want to get the brakes done on the wife's car, 161-L-2345."
Receptionist

02NCT

NCT-prep specifics

The AI knows the NCT calendar — the test is due 4-yearly until age 10, then yearly, based on first registration date. For a customer ringing "my NCT is in 3 weeks," the AI confirms the exact due date from RSA data and books a pre-check at the right time. Not too early (waste), not too late (leaves no time to fix).

03Quote

When it's not a quotable job

Half of caller queries are "how much to fix [vague description]" — a noise, a warning light, intermittent stalling. The AI doesn't make up prices. It books a diagnostic visit (€60 typically, deducted from final cost if work goes ahead) and tells the caller honestly the price will depend on what's found. No surprise bills, no quotes the mechanic can't honour.

§ 08 — FAQ

Mechanic questions we actually get

Written as they're asked — by independent-garage owners and lead mechanics. Answered honestly, including where this system is not the right fit.

01 Can it quote a service?

Quotes from your fixed-price menu (full service, interim, oil change, brake pads, timing belt, NCT pre-check) — yes. Fixed prices for fixed jobs are accurate every time.

Diagnostic-required jobs ("there's a noise from the front") — no. The AI doesn't invent prices. It books a diagnostic visit (€60 typically, deducted from any follow-on work) and explains the price will depend on what's found. Better one small awkward conversation than a customer arriving expecting a €120 bill and getting €450.

02 What about NCT pre-checks?

The AI knows your NCT pre-check service — typical price €60, 90 minutes, includes a written report. It captures the rego, looks up the NCT due date from RSA data, and books at the right time (3–4 weeks before NCT is ideal — leaves time to fix anything before the test).

Sends a prep checklist by SMS — bulbs, tyres, washer fluid, service-history book — so the customer arrives with the obvious stuff already sorted.

03 Will it route to me when I'm under a bonnet?

Only for genuine urgent calls — a customer's car is at the gate, a breakdown is time-critical. The AI uses tone-of-voice and keyword signals ("urgent," "waiting outside," "broken down"). For routine bookings, status check-ins, parts queries, it handles it without pulling you off a job.

On Growth, the AI can also escalate to a senior tech vs apprentice based on call type — keep the apprentice on routine bookings, page the senior only on diagnostic queries.

04 Does it integrate with my workshop diary?

Starter and Professional book into Google Calendar (the most common option for small independents). Growth integrates with workshop tools — Workshop Mate, Garage Hive, Autowork, GarageBox, ServiceBox — and ties bookings to customer records and service history. If you're on something we haven't wired, send a sample at onboarding and we'll scope it — usually 3–7 working days.

05 What about urgent breakdowns and towing?

The AI detects breakdown language — "won't start," "broken down," "side of the road," "AA on its way" — and switches to a different flow. Captures Eircode + nearest landmark, symptoms, and either pages you (if you handle towing yourself) or refers to your towing partner with the customer's details forwarded. They don't get bounced "let me check and call back."

06 Can I keep my existing garage number?

Yes. We don't take your number; we sit behind it. Set call forwarding on busy and no-answer (and after-hours) from your existing landline to a CallMeIE number — standard carrier feature. Number on the signage and on Google Maps stays unchanged.

Full porting available on Growth: we transfer via ComReg, takes about 10 working days.

07 GDPR — what's stored and for how long?

Call audio: 30 days, then deleted. Transcripts: 90 days, then deleted. Booking + service-history data is retained as long as your workshop diary retains it — we don't add a second copy. Caller phone numbers are stored only as long as the booking and reminder-SMS cycle requires.

DPA on request for Growth / Enterprise; standard processor terms with Starter / Professional. A caller can request erasure — we purge within 72 hours.
