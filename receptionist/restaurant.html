§ 02 — Capabilities

What it handles for a restaurant

Six receptionist jobs, done by one system, tuned for Irish hospitality patterns — bookings, allergens, private dining, takeaway, supplier deliveries, after-hours enquiries.

I — Bookings

Live table availability

Reads OpenTable, ResDiary, SevenRooms, or your in-house diary in real time. Quotes actual availability — never "let me check and ring you back." Books straight in. Flags duplicates if the same number tries to book twice.

II — Allergens

Allergen-14 matrix

Knows your full allergen-14 matrix dish-by-dish. Caller asks "can my coeliac partner eat the steak frites?" — the AI cross-references your matrix and answers correctly. Flags the booking so the chef sees it before service.

III — Private dining

Function & events

Captures party size, occasion (hen, work do, anniversary, christening), requested date, budget range, and dietary requirements. Routes to the events team or owner inbox with a structured brief — not a "call back" voicemail message.

IV — Takeaway

Phone takeaway orders

Reads the takeaway menu, takes orders by phone, totals the bill, captures pickup time. Writes to the kitchen printer or POS. Card payment over the phone is handled via your existing payment terminal — never to the AI directly (PCI clean).

V — After-hours

Closed-day enquiries

Mondays closed? The AI tells callers, takes Tuesday-onwards bookings, and lets a sommelier-quality private-dining enquiry through to your inbox. Doesn't lose Tuesday's wine-club booking just because Monday is closed.

VI — Weekly report

Monday morning summary

Plain email Monday: bookings taken, no-shows confirmed, cancellations, allergen flags raised, private-dining enquiries opened, calls outside opening hours. Read in ninety seconds — no dashboard.

§ 03 — Depth

Built for Irish hospitality specifically

The stuff a generic "global" booking bot gets wrong every time: Irish allergen-14 regulation (FSAI), Irish booking-deposit norms, the difference between a sit-down booking and a takeaway, the no-show problem in Irish casual dining.

01Allergens

FSAI allergen compliance, on the call

Under the FSAI's allergen rules, you must declare which of the 14 named allergens appear in each dish. The AI carries your live allergen-14 matrix — updated when you change the menu — and answers correctly on the call. No "ah I think the curry is gluten-free" guesses by the floor staff.

Caller
"My daughter's nut-allergic — is the chocolate fondant OK?"
Receptionist
The chocolate fondant doesn't contain nuts, but it's made in a kitchen that handles nuts elsewhere — there's a cross-contamination notice on the menu. Chef will check on the night. Want me to flag the booking?
02No-shows

Deposits + reminder SMS

For larger parties (8+) or weekend evening prime-time slots, the AI takes a deposit-card hold via your payment system — never directly. Sends a reminder SMS 24 hours before with a one-tap "confirm" / "cancel" / "change time" link. No-show rate drops 50% in the first month.

03Brand voice

It sounds like your restaurant, not "global"

Onboarding includes a 30-minute call to capture your tone — formal Michelin, casual gastropub, family-friendly, Italian-warm, Asian-precise. The AI is tuned to that tone. A Limerick neighbourhood bistro doesn't sound like an LA chain. The script uses your phrasing — "would yourselves prefer the back room?" not "would you all like the rear dining area?"

§ 08 — FAQ

Restaurant questions we actually get

Written as they're asked — by owner-operators and floor managers. Answered honestly, including where this system is not the right fit.

01 Does it know my allergen menu?

Yes — at onboarding we capture your full allergen-14 matrix, dish-by-dish. When you change the menu, we update it within 24 hours of receiving the new sheet (Growth tier) or weekly (Professional). The AI cross-references the caller's allergen with your matrix and answers honestly — including "the kitchen handles X but the dish doesn't contain it" cross-contamination flags.

For severe allergies (anaphylaxis-grade), the AI always defers to the chef on the night and flags the booking — no "yes that's fine" without a chef sign-off.

02 Can it take a takeaway order?

On Growth, yes — phone takeaway orders are read into the kitchen printer or POS (we wire to Lightspeed, Toast, Square, Revel). The AI quotes the menu, totals the bill, captures pickup time. Card payments go through your existing terminal on collection — never to the AI directly (PCI-clean, GDPR-clean).

On Starter / Professional, takeaway orders are emailed to the kitchen rather than POS-integrated.

03 What about late-night cancellations?

The AI is on 24/7 (Professional and Growth). Cancellations after closing time go through to OpenTable / ResDiary in real time, releasing the slot for a wait-list customer or web-booking the next day. No more "we tried to cancel but no one answered" frustration on TripAdvisor.

If the cancellation is for a deposit-hold booking, the AI explains the cancellation policy honestly (24-hour notice = full refund, less than 24 hours = deposit forfeited per your policy).

04 Will it sound like our brand?

On Growth, yes — onboarding includes a 30-minute call to capture your tone. We tune the AI to match: formal Michelin, casual gastropub, Italian-warm, family- friendly. We share three sample-call recordings before you go live, you sign off on the voice, and we tweak from there.

On Starter / Professional the voice is the default Irish-accented receptionist. It's polite and professional but not custom-tuned to your brand.

05 Does it integrate with OpenTable / ResDiary?

Yes — both, plus SevenRooms, Yumpingo, and a few others. Two-way live sync means the AI sees actual availability and writes the booking back. No double-bookings, no "we'll confirm by email" delays.

If you're on a system we haven't wired (in-house custom, COVERMANAGER, etc), send us the API docs at onboarding and we'll scope a connector — usually 3–7 working days.

06 Can I keep my existing restaurant number?

Yes. We don't take your number; we sit behind it. Set call forwarding on busy and no-answer (and after-hours) from your existing landline to a CallMeIE number — standard Eircom / Sky / Virgin feature. The number on Google Maps and your shopfront stays unchanged.

Full porting available on Growth: we transfer via ComReg, takes about 10 working days.

07 GDPR — what's stored and for how long?

Call audio: 30 days, then deleted. Transcripts: 90 days, then deleted. Booking data is retained as long as your OpenTable / ResDiary retains it — we don't add a second copy. Allergen flags persist with the booking. Caller phone numbers are stored only as long as the booking and reminder-SMS cycle requires.

DPA on request for Growth / Enterprise; standard processor terms with Starter / Professional. A caller can request erasure — we purge within 72 hours.
