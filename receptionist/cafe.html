§ 02 — Capabilities

What it handles for a café

Six receptionist jobs, done by one system, tuned for Irish café patterns — lunch-rush phones, today's bake, allergens, catering enquiries, supplier deliveries, seasonal hours.

I — Bake list

Today's-bake awareness

The baker sends through the morning bake list at open — sourdough, gluten-free, scones, cakes, brownies. The AI knows what's on the counter and what's sold out. Answers caller queries honestly without guessing.

II — Allergens

Allergen-14 matrix

Knows the allergen-14 matrix for every cake, sandwich, and special on the menu. Caller asks "is the carrot cake nut-free?" — the AI cross-references and answers correctly. Flags severe allergies for the chef on the day.

III — Catering

Catering & events intake

Captures party size, occasion (office breakfast, school cake order, christening finger-food), date, dietary mix, budget. Sends a structured brief to your inbox. You read it in 30 seconds at end-of-service — not a 3-minute voicemail.

IV — Suppliers

Delivery & loading-bay calls

Drivers ringing for the loading bay, the back-door access code, or "I'm five minutes away" — the AI handles it. Doesn't pull you off the till for a delivery confirmation. Notes new suppliers for you to call back later.

V — Hours

Seasonal & holiday hours

Knows your summer hours, winter hours, bank-holiday hours, Christmas closure. After- hours callers get tomorrow's opening time. Saves the "ah we're closed Christmas Eve afternoon" voicemail you don't want anyone leaving.

VI — Weekly report

Monday morning summary

Plain email Monday: catering enquiries, allergen flags, "do you have X" calls (intel on what to add to the menu), missed calls, supplier handoffs. Read in ninety seconds — no dashboard.

Built for Irish café trade specifically

For cafés in tourist towns (Adare, Kilkenny, Doolin, Dingle, Killarney), summer hours differ from winter by hours, not minutes. The AI knows your seasonal calendar. A summer 8am-9pm answer in January would lose you trust on Trustpilot. The system updates with your calendar — no surprise late-night Tuesday-in-November confusion.

§ 06 — Irish

Built for Ireland, not for "UK & Ireland"

The specifics that matter on a café call — FSAI allergen-14 compliance, Irish summer tourist hours, "today's bake" culture, regional accents, the gap between certified gluten-free and "we do gluten-free."

  • Allergen-14 (FSAI)The Irish FSAI requires the 14 named allergens to be declared per dish. The AI carries your live matrix and answers honestly on the call.
  • Today's bakeThe Irish café convention — a daily-changing bake — is built in. Baker SMSes the morning's list; AI knows what's available.
  • Tourist-town hoursSummer hours can run 8am–9pm in Doolin, winter 9am–5pm. AI follows your seasonal calendar, not a static "open until 5."
  • Bank HolidaysKnows the Irish bank-holiday calendar. Quotes BH hours correctly. Knows St. Patrick's Day, Easter Monday, June bank holiday demand.
  • Certified vs catering-styleKnows the difference between Coeliac Society of Ireland-accredited and "we do gluten-free options" — answers honestly to avoid Trustpilot grief.
  • Irish numbersAccepts +353 mobile and landline. 085, 086, 087, 089 prefixes interchangeably.
  • Accents testedMunster, Ulster, Leinster, Connacht — tested against real caller recordings. International tourist accents (US, French, German, Spanish) also handled.

§ 08 — FAQ

Café questions we actually get

Written as they're asked — by café owners and head baristas. Answered honestly, including where this system is not the right fit.

01 Can it tell people what's in the cake today?

Yes — and it's the most useful single thing for a busy café. The baker sends today's bake list by SMS at open ("8 sourdough, 4 GF brownies, 2 vegan scones, lemon drizzle on the counter"). The AI carries the list and updates as the day goes — customers get accurate "yes we have / sold out by 1" answers without you stopping on the till.

On Growth, the bake list integrates directly with your Square or Lightspeed inventory — auto-decrements as items sell.

02 Does it know our hours change in summer?

Yes — your seasonal calendar (summer hours, winter hours, bank-holiday hours, Christmas closure, festival weekend hours) all get loaded at onboarding and updated by SMS or shared calendar. After-hours callers get the next opening time correctly. No "open till 5" answer in January when you actually closed at 4 in winter.

03 What about catering enquiries?

This is the high-value one for a café. The AI captures the brief — party size, occasion (office breakfast, school cake, baby shower, christening), date, dietary mix, budget — and emails it to you structured. You read it in 30 seconds at end-of-service and ring back with a quote at 3pm.

Compared to a voicemail message that says "hi I was wondering about catering, can you ring me back" — the structured brief is a ten-times better starting point for the call back.

04 Will my regulars know it's not me on the phone?

Most don't notice on the first call — the voice is Irish-accented and answers are practical. The AI identifies itself as "CallMeIE" if asked directly ("am I talking to a real person?") — we don't deceive callers.

Most regulars figure out it's automated by call three or four. The feedback we get is consistent: they prefer it because the call is answered, the bake list is accurate, and they don't have to ring back.

05 Does it integrate with Square / Lightspeed?

On Growth — yes, both. Phone pre-orders write to the till, inventory decrements, payment is taken at collection on the existing terminal (PCI-clean). On Starter and Professional, pre-orders are emailed to the owner / counter for manual entry.

We also wire to Toast, Revel, Clover, and a few others. If you're on something we haven't connected, send the API docs at onboarding.

06 Can I keep my existing café number?

Yes. We don't take your number; we sit behind it. Set call forwarding on busy and no-answer (and after-hours) from your existing landline to a CallMeIE number — standard Eircom / Sky / Virgin feature. Number on Google Maps and your sandwich board stays unchanged.

Full porting available on Growth: we transfer via ComReg, takes about 10 working days.

07 GDPR — what's stored and for how long?

Call audio: 30 days, then deleted. Transcripts: 90 days, then deleted. Catering- enquiry briefs are kept as long as the booking is open + 6 months for paperwork compliance. A caller can request erasure — we purge within 72 hours.

DPA on request for Growth / Enterprise; standard processor terms with Starter / Professional.
