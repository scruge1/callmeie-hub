Estimating the cost of missed calls

Use your call records to estimate the enquiry workload and possible revenue opportunity.

By Adam Vaughan  ·  Founder, CallMeIE  ·  Limerick, Ireland  ·  Updated 17 May 2026

Missed calls are not all lost sales. Use your own records and state the assumptions in your estimate.

Use your own call records

A missed call can be a new enquiry, an existing customer, a supplier or a wrong number. It does not always represent lost revenue. Review a typical week of missed calls and callbacks before estimating the impact.

An illustrative calculation

Estimated missed enquiries × your usual conversion rate × average job value gives a possible revenue opportunity. It is an estimate, not a measurement of lost sales.

For example: 8 missed calls × 0.6 enquiry share × 0.4 conversion rate × €180 average job value = about €346 per week. Each input is an assumption. Replace it with your own figures and account for callers you reach later.

Options for handling calls

  • Schedule callbacks and tell callers when to expect a reply.
  • Use staff or an answering service where the workload supports it.
  • Configure an AI receptionist to answer approved questions and follow an agreed booking or callback workflow.

The appropriate option depends on call volume, working hours and the requests your business receives. Automation does not guarantee bookings or revenue.

There is no single figure. Use your own missed-call records, enquiry share, conversion rate and job value. Treat the result as an estimate.
