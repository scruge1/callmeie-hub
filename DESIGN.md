# CallMeIE design direction

Use original6578619 as the visual baseline. The owner liked that site and
asked for less copy, consistent typography, working thumbnails and alignment.
The replacement phone scenes and new identity were rejected.

Preserve the original warm paper, ink/navy, serif display headings, section
rules and layouts. Keep the original Websites orange accent and Receptionist
editorial treatment. Use Fraunces for display and Inter for supporting text.
Different heading sizes are valid hierarchy; do not flatten them into one size.

Lead with an Irish AI phone receptionist for Irish businesses. Websites are
a secondary product. Explain the practical offer briefly. Remove repeated
explanations, founder narration, obsolete DocOps offers and unsupported numbers.
Preserve material prices, terms, coverage hours, consent and legal information.
Do not imply that bookings, transfers or integrations are included in every plan.

Use actual thumbnail captures for website concepts. Keep labels honest about
illustrative demos. Preserve useful functional improvements from the later
release, including the custom sample player, without importing the redesign.

Check every page visually at mobile and desktop widths. Check document
overflow, local table scrolling, open menus, enlarged text and working controls.
Inspect flagged geometry before treating hidden menus or decoration as defects.
Keep private screenshots outside the repository.

Generated assets, page textures and3D are a later review stage. Add an asset
only when it improves a specific identified part of the established design.
