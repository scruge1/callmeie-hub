Care vs Managed — pick the one that matches your situation

Care (this page) is for customers who already have a website and only want it looked after. €45 / €95 / €195/mo. Cancel any month with 30 days' notice. No build included.

Managed is for customers who want us to build AND maintain a new site for a fixed monthly fee, €0 upfront. €69 / €100 / €149/mo with a 12 or 18-month minimum term (because the build is fronted). Source code stays with us during the term.

Common questions

Do I need to be on a Care plan to get my site looked after?

No, but it's a lot cheaper than one-off hourly. A Care plan caps your monthly cost, keeps hosting/security/backups inside the fee, and gives you a predictable response SLA. One-off hourly is €60/hr with no SLA.

What counts as "an edit to existing content"?

Phone number change, opening-hours update, swap a photo, fix a typo, price change, add a Christmas-hours banner. Anything that touches text or media that's already on the site. New pages, new sections, new branding, new integrations are NEW design work and get a separate quote.

How do you stop the bill quietly creeping up?

We email you BEFORE going over your monthly budget and ask what you want to do — "do it" (billed at €60/hr) or "wait until next month" (deferred). Time is tracked in 15-minute increments, rounded up to the nearest 5 minutes, and itemised on your monthly invoice. No silent overage charges.

What's the difference between Essential's "30 min/mo" and Growth's "2 hr/mo"?

Same scope (edits to existing content), bigger time budget. Growth is for sites with regular content changes — new blog posts, seasonal banners, occasional new staff bios. Essential is for sites that mostly sit still and just need the lights kept on.

What does Concierge "unlimited" actually mean?

"Unlimited" means you stop having to count. The soft cap is 6 hours/month on a rolling 90-day average, with an 8-hour single-month ceiling. If you cross the soft cap two months running, we have a friendly conversation — not a bill — about whether your usage has changed enough to need a custom retainer. We have never asked anyone to upgrade in the first 12 months on Concierge.

Can I switch between tiers?

Yes — month-to-month. Email support@callmeie.ie and the new tier kicks in next billing cycle. No upgrade or downgrade fees.

What if I cancel?

30 days' written notice to support@callmeie.ie. We provide a static export of your live site content within 7 days. If your site is hosted with us, we'll give you a portable copy plus DNS-handover instructions.

I want a new site too — should I take Care or Managed?

If you already have a site, take Care. If you don't have a site yet, Managed bundles the build + the maintenance for a single monthly fee at €0 upfront — cheaper than buying a one-off Build and then adding Care on top.

Not sure which plan fits?

Forward us your site URL and a one-line description of how often things change. We'll reply within 24 hours with a tier recommendation and a sample monthly time budget.
