---

## Why this file exists

GDPR Article 30 obliges controllers and processors to maintain internal records of processing activities. Ireland abolished the controller/processor registration scheme on 25 May 2018 — there is **no DPC registration** to file, but the ROPA must exist on demand for any DPC inspection or customer DPA audit (per `legal/dpa.html` and `_research/2026-05-06-ie-business-accounting.md` Q6).

This is the canonical record. Update in this file, in the same commit as any change to processing flows. No spreadsheet, no Notion, no Google Doc — keep it in the repo so the change history IS the audit log.

---

## Format

One row per distinct processing activity. Fields per Article 30(1) for controller activities; Article 30(2) for processor activities; we mark which.

| Activity | Role | Categories of data subjects | Categories of personal data | Purpose | Legal basis (Art 6) | Recipients / sub-processors | Retention | Cross-border transfers | Security measures |

## Processor activities (CallMeIE Technologies Ltd as processor — for customer's data)

### P1. Document Ops — invoice/receipt extraction

- **Controller:** customer (the Irish SMB or bookkeeping practice)
- **Categories of data subjects:** customer's suppliers, customer's customers (where invoiced)
- **Categories of personal data:** vendor name, address, VAT number, IBAN, contact email, line-item descriptions, totals + VAT amounts, dates; potentially personal names where invoices are billed to individuals
- **Purpose of processing:** OCR + extraction + bounce-back queue + clean-export CSV per customer's instruction
- **Legal basis at controller level:** Article 6(1)(b) contract / 6(1)(c) statutory bookkeeping — controller's basis, not ours
- **Sub-processors (per `legal/dpa.html` Schedule B):** Hetzner Online GmbH (Nuremberg DE — hosting + Object Storage); HumanSignal Inc. (Label Studio — self-hosted on our Hetzner box, no data egress); Stripe (billing only, no invoice content); Resend (transactional email — magic link, bounce notifications); Anthropic PBC (US — verifier model API, *single-field-snippet only*, no full-doc payload egress)
- **Retention:** original-form documents kept for the period in the customer's Order Form; default 6 years (VATCA s.84(3)); cleared 30 days post-cancellation (`scripts/permanent_delete.py`)
- **Cross-border:** Anthropic API US — covered by SCCs in our DPA; Stripe US for some payment processing
- **Security measures:** TLS 1.3 in transit; AES-256 at rest in Hetzner Object Storage; per-tenant prefix isolation enforced server-side per `infra/HETZNER-OBJECT-STORAGE-ACCESS-PATTERN.md`; magic-link login (15-min TTL, sha256-hashed); audit log on every access; alembic migration 0003 stripe_events idempotency + suppressed_at deletion clock

### P2. AI Receptionist — voice + booking

- **Controller:** customer (the Irish SMB)
- **Categories of data subjects:** callers to the customer's phone number
- **Categories of personal data:** caller phone number, caller name (if spoken), call recording audio, transcript text, calendar event details, SMS confirmation content
- **Purpose of processing:** answer inbound calls, book appointments to customer's Google Calendar, send confirmation SMS, deliver weekly call summary
- **Legal basis at controller level:** Article 6(1)(b) contract — caller is engaging customer's business; recording disclosed at call start (ePrivacy 2011)
- **Sub-processors (per `legal/dpa.html` Schedule A):** Vapi Inc. (US — voice runtime + recording — caveat: not EU-resident, US infrastructure); Twilio Inc. (US/IE — telephony + SMS); Google LLC Workspace (Calendar API — EU residency where customer set); Anthropic PBC (US — Haiku 4.5 anomaly diagnosis on failed calls only); Resend (transactional email summaries); Render Inc. - **Retention:** call audio purged at 30 days post-call (90-day customer-retention period reduced for privacy); transcripts purged at 90 days; booking history retained for customer-relationship lifetime + 30 days post-cancellation
- **Cross-border:** Vapi US (SCCs in DPA); Anthropic US (SCCs); Twilio US/IE (SCCs); Google Workspace EU residency where set
- **Security measures:** prompt-disclosure validator (regex check on system prompt for "may be recorded" — fail-closed at every Vapi /assistant create or PATCH per `setup-new-client.py:assert_recording_disclosure`); per-call hard cap 600s on Vapi to prevent cost runaway; HMAC-verified webhook from Vapi; `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` env-only (no client exposure)

### P3. AI-First Websites — hosting + Care plan ops

- **Controller:** customer (the Irish SMB)
- **Categories of data subjects:** customer's site visitors

## Activities NOT performed (negative control)

- **No marketing emailing without consent.** No bulk newsletter, no list rental, no sale of customer or prospect data. Article 6(1)(a) consent path is not in use.
- **No automated decision-making or profiling under Article 22.** Doc Ops is decision-support only — bookkeeper is the decision-maker. AI Receptionist follows pre-configured rules; no profiling of callers.
- **No special-category data (Article 9) processed.** Customer GDPR exempt-medical Doc Ops invoices contain VAT exempt status as a data field but no health-record content; this is non-special data per WP29 guidance.
- **No children's data (Article 8).** All customer-facing services are B2B.
