How it works.

Four steps. No new accounting software. Plugs in front of Sage, Xero, BrightBooks or QuickBooks.

01

Upload

Drag-drop PDFs, scans or photos into your tenant inbox. Or email them. Or pipe them from Klippa's output.

02

Extract

OCR reads every field. We score how sure we are about each one. If we're 98%-sure across the important bits, the doc goes straight through. If not, it gets held back.

03

Review

Held-back docs come back to you with a one-line reason — usually one of: blurry photo, handwriting, two invoices in one PDF. On Rescue + Export and Bespoke plans, Adam handles those docs personally instead.

04

Export

Download CSV. Paste into Sage, Xero, BrightBooks or QuickBooks. Done in minutes, not hours.

§ 05 — Five jobs, five paths

Five real-world batches running through the same pipeline.

Same pipeline. Five shapes. Each shows what enters, where the confidence gate fires, what bounces back to you, and what the customer sees on the other side.

§ 05a

Bookkeeping firm · mixed monthly batch

A two-bookkeeper Cork practice closes 80 docs from one client folder every month-end. The bookkeeper wants the run done by Wednesday lunchtime so Friday's payroll work has air.

What enters

80 PDFs and JPGs, mixed: 65 standard supplier invoices (UK + IE + EU including 4 GBP and 2 EUR-equivalent multi-currency), 5 credit notes, 3 supplier statements (not invoices), 2 multi-invoice PDFs, 2 duplicate invoices, 1 non-invoice attachment (a courier delivery slip), 1 mixed-VAT invoice (23% + 13.5% same supplier), 1 cross-border reverse-charge invoice from a UK SaaS vendor.

What the customer sees

A 71-row CSV pre-mapped to Sage Business Cloud Accounting (vendor / invoice number / date / net / VAT-23 / VAT-13.5 / VAT-0-RC / total / currency / EUR-equivalent / notes). Plus a quarantine list: 9 filenames with re-submit reasons. Bookkeeper splits the 2 multi-invoice PDFs in Acrobat (yields 6 fresh invoices), confirms the duplicate detection, drops the 6 split invoices back. End state: 77 rows in Sage by 09:35. The 3 supplier statements + 1 delivery slip stay out of the books.

Honest caveats. Auto Plus does not check whether credit notes are correct — only that they extracted cleanly. Pipeline excludes time spent reconciling foreign-currency rate-dates against tax-points (bookkeeper still owns that reconciliation). Pipeline does not chase missing receipts.

Dental clinic owner · quarterly batch

A solo-dentist practice in Galway closes the quarter on the last Friday. The owner wants the books square before the accountant's Monday call. Forty docs sit in the inbox.

What enters

40 PDFs: 22 supplier invoices (Henry Schein, Optident, lab fees from two local labs), 18 patient billing aggregations (the practice management system exports a weekly patient-revenue summary as a PDF). All standard layout, all sub-€2,000.

What the customer sees

A 35-row CSV mapped to BrightBooks supplier-invoice import. Plus a 5-doc quarantine list with crop thumbnails for the 2 lab-fee invoices and a small "re-categorise as journal" note on the 3 revenue summaries. Owner re-photographs the two lab invoices over the weekend, drops them back Monday morning, gets 37 clean rows. The 3 revenue summaries get keyed as a single journal manually.

Honest caveats. Auto tier does not understand HSE Dental Treatment Services billing rules. It does not validate whether the invoiced VAT rate is correct for dental services (mostly exempt; some materials at 23%) — it extracts what is on the page. Owner spot-checks before paste.

§ 05d

Freight forwarder · Bespoke tier

A small forwarder in Dublin Port runs ~200 docs/month across BOL + CMR + customs declarations + supplier invoices. Compliance officer wants Revenue-defensible records and a TMS feed that does not break on multi-leg routing. Not a self-serve scenario.

What enters

200 docs/month, mixed: BOL + CMR (carrier-specific layouts, often 6 different carriers in a week), customs declaration printouts (Revenue ROS), supplier invoices for warehouse + groupage + customs broker fees, driver expense sheets including handwritten items. Multi-leg routing on ~30% of consignments means one shipment generates 3-4 linked docs.

What the customer sees

A live TMS feed (or custom CSV per the scoping call) where ~70% of docs land same-day clean, 30% land within five business days reviewed by name. A monthly summary email signed by Adam. A waitlist position (1-2 slots only, full disclosure) if they came in after capacity was claimed.

Honest caveats. The 5-business-day SLA is real and load-bearing. If you need T+1 turnaround on customs paperwork because a container is sitting at the port, this tier cannot promise it — that is a phone call, not a pipeline. We do not handle live container tracking. We do not file customs declarations on your behalf. We extract and feed the system that does.

§ 05e

Construction subcontractor · RCT reverse-charge

A plumbing subcontractor in Tipperary invoices a principal contractor on RCT (Relevant Contracts Tax — Revenue's construction reverse-charge regime). Standard OCR pipelines treat the missing VAT line as a missing field. Sage rejects the import. The subbie's bookkeeper has been hand-coding these for two years.

What enters

12 invoices per fortnight: 8 standard supplier invoices (merchant — Chadwicks / Heiton / Brooks), 4 RCT invoices the subbie issues to the principal contractor (these get re-imported into the subbie's own bookkeeping for the receivables side, with deduction authorisation / RCTDC details supplied by the principal contractor where tax was deducted).

What the customer sees

A 12-row CSV that pastes into Sage Business Cloud Accounting's RCT-aware import without error. The bookkeeper opens Sage and the rows are coded correctly the first time. Compare: previous Dext run flagged all four RCT invoices as "VAT missing" and required manual re-coding — ~8 minutes per doc, every fortnight.

Honest caveats. The validator handles standard RCT notation as published by Revenue. Non-standard wording on the principal contractor's RCTDC printout — Revenue updates the format from time to time — can still flag against the planning 15% bounce-back rate. We update the validator within two business days of any Revenue format change. We do not file RCT returns. We do not generate RCTDC certificates. We extract them correctly so Sage does not reject them.

Common questions.

"We already use Klippa / AutoEntry / Hubdoc / Dext."

Good — keep using them. Document Ops sits between your OCR tool and your accounting software. We only let rows through if we're 98% sure they're right. If we're not sure, the doc comes back to you with one line saying why. On the Rescue + Export and Bespoke plans, Adam handles those doubtful ones for you.

"How does this compare to a bookkeeper?"

Document Ops is not a bookkeeper. It's the data-entry-and-export layer in front of one. Self-serve tiers automate the typing and quarantine the doubtful docs back to you. Bespoke tier scopes around a specific operating workflow (freight, customs, contracts) where Adam personally reviews the residual queue. Pricing reflects that — Self-serve is workflow-tool pricing; Bespoke is a small concierge engagement.

"What if your extraction is wrong?"

We only auto-export when we're 98% sure on the important bits — vendor, total, VAT split, date. If anything looks wrong, the doc comes back to you with a one-line reason. It never quietly lands in your books. The Self-serve plans don't include any human review — the bounce-back is the safety net. The Rescue + Export plan adds 10 docs per month where Adam personally cleans up the ambiguous ones; anything beyond that bounces back as normal. You still spot-check the CSV before pasting — that's on you. We're upfront about what the pipeline doesn't handle: handwritten dockets, two invoices in one PDF, mixed VAT, and RCT formats Revenue has just changed.

"Where does our data live?"

Coolify on a Hetzner box in Nuremberg (EU). GDPR compliant. Multi-tenant Postgres with strict tenant_id isolation enforced at the middleware layer. Documents auto-deleted after the retention window in your contract.

"Can we cancel anytime?"

Yes. Pilots are one-off. Monthly retainers are month-to-month, no lock-in. Stripe handles billing — cancel from your customer portal.

"Do you do freight / property / law?"

Bookkeeping firms first. Freight forwarders next (BOL / CMR / customs). Property management third (maintenance invoices + lease forms). Solicitors after a reference client signs. If your vertical isn't listed, email us — we'll be honest about whether it's a fit yet.
