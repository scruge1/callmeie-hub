1 · Roles

Until Callmeie Technologies Ltd is incorporated and CRO-listed, the Processor is Adam Vaughan trading as Callmeie / Callmeie Technologies, an Irish VAT-registered sole trader operating in Limerick, Ireland. Upon incorporation, Adam Vaughan may assign and novate this DPA to Callmeie Technologies Ltd, provided the company assumes all Processor obligations in writing and the Controller receives written notice within 30 days of incorporation.

The Controller (you) determines purposes and means of processing the personal data contained in documents submitted to Document Ops. The Processor processes such personal data solely on the Controller's documented instructions, per the DPA, the MSA, and the Order Form.

2 · Scope

  • Subject matter: automated extraction of structured data from accounting source documents (invoices, receipts, credit notes, statements) supplied by the Controller.
  • Duration: term of the active Order Form, plus any post-termination retention period set in §8.
  • Nature: OCR, field extraction, confidence scoring, optional human review, export to the Controller's accounting software or download.
  • Purpose: enabling the Controller to meet bookkeeping and tax obligations under Irish Revenue rules and equivalent regimes.
  • Legal basis: Article 28 GDPR + Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

3 · Where your data lives

  • Coolify on a Hetzner box in Nuremberg, Germany (EU jurisdiction).
  • Multi-tenant Postgres with strict tenant_id isolation enforced at the middleware layer.
  • Object storage for original-form documents at Hetzner Object Storage (EU, S3-compatible).
  • Backups encrypted at rest, retained per the Order Form retention window.
  • No transfers outside the EEA without an explicit additional clause.

4 · Cancellation + data export

You may cancel a monthly subscription at any time from the Stripe customer portal. Pilots are one-off and do not auto-renew. On cancellation:

  • Within 7 calendar days we deliver: a full CSV export of all extracted rows + a ZIP of the original-form documents.
  • Within 30 days (or sooner on written request) we permanently delete all your data from primary storage + backups.
  • We retain nothing past the contractual retention window.

5 · Sub-processors

Current sub-processor list (full register on request):

  • Hetzner Online GmbH · hosting + object storage · Nuremberg, DE
  • Stripe Payments Europe Ltd · payment processing · Dublin, IE
  • Cloudflare Inc. · DNS + CDN · with EU-resident routing where available
  • HumanSignal Inc. (Label Studio) · review tooling · self-hosted on the Hetzner box (no data egress)

Any change to the sub-processor list triggers 30-day prior written notice, with a Controller objection right.

6 · Security measures

  • TLS 1.3 in transit (Cloudflare-managed).
  • Encryption at rest for backups + object storage.
  • Per-tenant database isolation enforced at the application middleware layer.
  • Magic-link authentication; no shared accounts; admin actions audit-logged.
  • Annual review of technical + organisational measures.

7 · Liability + indemnity

Per the Master Service Agreement. Self-serve tier liability is capped per the published contract; Bespoke engagements negotiate a per-engagement liability cap during the scoping call. We carry professional indemnity insurance (carrier + cover details available on request as part of the full DPA).
