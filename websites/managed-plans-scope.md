**Last updated:** 2026-05-19.
**Customer-facing:** yes. Plain language. Lives at `/websites/managed-plans-scope`.

Same maintenance rules, different starting point. If you want to own the code outright, take the one-off "own it outright" Build instead — see below.

## The simple rule (identical to our Care plans)

The maintenance scope rule is exactly the one on our [Care Plans page](/websites/care-plans-scope) — we do not restate it differently here, on purpose:

**Edits to existing content = included, up to your tier's monthly time budget. New design/build work = quoted at €60/hour, started only after you reply `do it`. We never bill silently — if a request would exceed your budget, we email you first.**

---

## What "Managed" adds on top of maintenance

- **The build is included.** You don't pay €695–€2,950 upfront for the site. We build it, then host + maintain it, for the monthly fee.
- **€0 onboarding.** Nothing to pay on signature. First month is billed when the site goes live.
- **We host and keep the code for the term.** Managed is a managed service — the source code stays with us while you're on the plan. You are not buying the code; you are buying a working, looked-after website. (Want the code in your own hands? That's the one-off **"own it outright"** Build — €695 / €1,595 / from €2,950, source yours, no monthly fee.)
- **Minimum term**, because the build is fronted at €0 (see each tier).

## Minimum term & cancelling

- The site is built at **€0 upfront**. The minimum term is simply how that build cost is recovered over time.
- **Cancel before the minimum term:** the remaining minimum-term monthly fees become payable in one final invoice. (No separate "kill fee" — there was no deposit to begin with.)
- **After the minimum term:** month-to-month. Cancel any time with **30 days' written notice** to `support@callmeie.ie`. No auto-renew traps.
- On cancellation we provide a static export of your live site content within 7 days, and full data handling per the agreement (`order-form-template.md` §G). Managed plans do **not** include a source-code repo handover — that's the one-off Build offer.

## Need a bigger change?

Same as Care: you don't upgrade your tier for a one-off. We quote it at **€60/hr** (€55/hr on Premium) — written quote within 1 business day, work starts after you reply `do it`. Email `support@callmeie.ie`.
