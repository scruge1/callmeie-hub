Care Plans — what's included, what's not

For: customers on Essential / Growth / Concierge monthly Care plans alongside an AI-First Websites build.  ·  Last updated: 2026-05-07.  ·  Why this exists: the most common reason agency Care plans turn into scope-creep losses is unclear scope. This page makes the boundary explicit so neither side feels surprised.

The simple rule

Edits to existing content (text, photos, hours, phone, prices) = included in your monthly plan, up to the time-budget for your tier.

New design work (new pages, new sections, branding, copywriting, photography) = quoted separately at €60/hour, billed only after you write back do it.

We never bill silently. If a request would exceed your monthly budget, we email you BEFORE doing the work and ask which option you prefer.

Essential — €45/month or €450/year (save 2 months)

Included

  • Hosting, SSL, DNS, daily backups
  • Uptime monitoring (5-minute checks; alert + emailed incident summary if >30 min outage)
  • Framework + dependency security patches within 7 days of disclosure
  • 30 minutes per month of edits to existing content — typo fixes, phone-number swap, opening-hours change, hero-image swap, price update
  • One emailed monthly summary: uptime %, top traffic pages, one suggestion

Out of scope (quoted at €60/hour)

  • New pages, new sections
  • Net-new design work (new colour scheme, new layout, new components)
  • Net-new copywriting (anything beyond fixing what's already written)
  • Photography or stock-image curation
  • Third-party integration (booking system, payment widget, Mailchimp embed)
  • SEO content (blog post, landing page targeting a specific search term)
  • Speed/performance optimisation projects (one-off audits OK; multi-day rewrites scoped)

Response SLA

2 business days from email landing in support@callmeie.ie to acknowledgement.

Change-request budget rules

  • 30 min/month, no rollover (unused minutes don't bank)
  • We email you BEFORE going over budget; you say do it (billed at €60/hr) or wait until next month (deferred)
  • Time tracked in 15-minute increments; rounded up to nearest 5 minutes
  • Documented monthly in your bill

Growth — €95/month or €950/year (save 2 months)

Includes everything in Essential, plus:

  • 2 hours per month of content updates (new blog post, special-offer page, seasonal campaign banner)
  • 1 new page per quarter (within the existing design system — e.g. a new service page, a new team-member bio)
  • Quarterly Lighthouse + broken-link scan; fixes shipped within 1 working week
  • Quarterly schema/meta SEO sweep
  • Google Business Profile sync (hours, photos, posts mirrored from your site)
  • Monthly 1-page report: Plausible/Umami traffic, top pages, contact-form volume, one recommended next fix

Out of scope (quoted at €60/hour)

Same as Essential — net-new design, branding, e-commerce builds, custom integrations, photography day-rate.

Response SLA

1 business day (8 working hours).

Change-request budget rules

  • 2 hr/month, with 30-minute rollover up to a 4-hour cap
  • Same do it / wait until next month rule for overages
  • Same 15-minute increment tracking

Concierge — €195/month or €1,950/year (save 2 months)

Includes everything in Growth, plus:

  • Unlimited fair-use edits — you forward emails, photos, screenshots; we do the work
  • We draft + publish: 2 blog posts per month, all GBP posts, seasonal offers, announcement banners
  • Monthly GBP photo refresh (we ask for a new photo each month, or refresh the featured image)
  • Contact-form triage: we read every form submission within 1 business day, reply or route to you with notes
  • Monthly 20-minute planning call (Google Meet)
  • Quarterly accessibility audit (axe-core + manual keyboard-nav check)
  • Annual full performance audit + remediation

Fair-use definition (the only "small print")

"Unlimited" means you stop having to count. The soft cap is 6 hours per month rolling 90-day average, with an 8-hour single-month ceiling. If you cross the soft cap two months running, we have a friendly conversation — not a bill — about whether your shape has changed enough to need a custom retainer. We have never asked anyone to upgrade in the first 12 months on Concierge.

Out of scope (quoted at €55/hour — €5/hr Concierge discount on top of the included scope)

  • Net-new branding (logo redesign, colour-system overhaul, new typeface licence)
  • E-commerce build (Stripe checkout flow, product catalogue) — this is a Custom-tier engagement
  • Photography day rate (€350/half-day on the road)
  • Custom integration builds (Zapier-style work, third-party API connectors)

Response SLA

Same business day for messages received before 14:00. Next morning otherwise.

What every Care plan includes by default

  • Email sent in plain English, not jargon
  • Every change shows up on the live site within minutes of you confirming, not days
  • Every change goes into your Git repo, so the history is yours forever
  • Every month you get one report — never more than one page
  • Cancel any time with 30 days' notice; no auto-renew traps

Where the time goes — three real examples

Essential customer, typical month (≈30 minutes used)

  • "Phone number changed to +353-86-XXX-XXXX" → 8 minutes (1 file, 1 commit, GBP API sync)
  • "Typo in hero — should be 'Open Mon-Sat'" → 5 minutes
  • "Christmas hours notice for 2 weeks" → 17 minutes (date-bounded banner with auto-expire)

Growth customer, typical month (≈90 minutes used of 2-hour budget)

  • 1 new blog post drafted-by-them, formatted by us, scheduled and published → 35 minutes
  • "Add a new staff member to /about" — copy + photo + bio → 45 minutes
  • 4 small text fixes across 3 pages → 10 minutes

Concierge customer, typical month (≈4 hours used; well under 6h soft cap)

  • 2 blog posts drafted by us → 1 hour 20 minutes
  • Monthly GBP post + photo refresh → 25 minutes
  • 7 small text/price/photo updates → 40 minutes
  • 4 contact-form replies routed to owner with our notes → 30 minutes
  • Monthly 20-minute planning call + 15 minutes prep → 35 minutes
  • 1 quarter-end Lighthouse fix shipped → 30 minutes
