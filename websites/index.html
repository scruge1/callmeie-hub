Common questions

Do I own the website?

Managed plans include hosting and maintenance; source code stays with us while you subscribe. With a one-off build, source code is handed to you. Included hosting varies by build tier.

Are the demos real client websites?

They are design concepts with illustrative businesses, names and details. They show directions we can adapt.

Can you connect booking or other tools?

We agree integrations during scope review. AI Receptionist and chatbot services are separate products and are quoted separately.
