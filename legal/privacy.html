2 · Information and purposes

CallMeIE's own enquiries and customers

When you contact us or use a contact or discovery form, we receive the information you provide, such as name, contact details, business information and your request. We use it to answer you and manage the enquiry. Where you are entering a contract as an individual, necessary pre-contract steps or performance can rely on Article 6(1)(b). For business-contact administration we rely on legitimate interests in responding and operating the service. Billing and tax records are also processed to meet legal obligations.

Providing enquiry details is voluntary, but we need enough information to answer or supply the requested service. Necessary network/security information is processed for service operation and abuse prevention. Optional website analytics uses your consent as described in §7; withdrawing it does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Information processed for business customers

  • AI Receptionist: incoming number, caller-provided details, audio/transcript where configured, routing and call outcomes, and agreed booking/message records.
  • Document Ops: uploaded documents, extracted fields, review corrections and access identifiers.
  • Websites: form contents, service/security logs and enabled analytics or payment information.

These operations are limited by the customer's instructions and service configuration. The business customer determines its lawful basis; it is not automatically contract just because it purchases our service. Recording, sensitive information and optional integrations require specific arrangements. Retention criteria are in §4.

3 · Hosting and processing locations

The receptionist backend uses Hetzner hosting in Germany. Voice, telephony, messaging and other configured providers can process data in other countries. The location of our database or archive does not establish the location of the entire processing chain.

Provider roles are described in §5. Customer-specific providers and processing locations are recorded with the DPA before processing. We do not describe the full service as EU-only.

4 · Retention

For CallMeIE's own enquiries, we keep the information needed to answer the enquiry and manage any resulting customer relationship. We review continued need when an enquiry closes or the relationship ends. Billing and tax records are kept for the applicable statutory period; security records are kept where needed to investigate incidents or abuse.

For customer-controlled information, retention follows the customer's documented instructions and agreed service settings, subject to applicable legal requirements. Audio, transcripts, call metadata and backups can have different periods. Customer settings are not inferred from the demo/admin archive.

A storage lifecycle rule does not establish deletion of every provider copy, log or backup. Requests to delete or return data are handled under §6 and the DPA; this notice does not promise a blanket twenty-four-hour or seventy-two-hour complete erasure.

6 · Your rights

Contact hello@callmeie.ie to request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, or to object, where the relevant GDPR conditions apply. You can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may need information to verify your identity, but will not request unnecessary information.

We respond without undue delay and normally within one month of receipt. Where Article 12 permits an extension for complexity or number of requests, we inform you within that first month, with the reasons. Legal retention requirements and the rights of others can affect what can be provided or erased.

If we act as Processor for a business customer, we pass the request to that Controller and assist it as required by the DPA. We do not use a thirty-day soft-delete period as a supposed Article 19 grace period.

You can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission. If a service involves decisions within Article 22, applicable safeguards must be addressed; this notice does not authorise significant automated decisions about you.

7 · Cookies + tracking

On callmeie.ie (this hub):

  • Optional analytics: accepting Allow page counts loads self-hosted Umami from analytics.owlzone.trade to count visits and selected interactions. Declining prevents this analytics script from loading. The browser stores your choice as cmt-consent. This notice does not certify anonymity of all network or hosting logs.
  • Cloudflare may set a strictly-necessary cookie for DDoS protection (__cf_bm, ~30 minutes).
  • Google Fonts loaded from fonts.googleapis.com + fonts.gstatic.com — no cookies set, but Google receives the visitor IP per request.

This stops the optional analytics script on reload. You can change a stored choice by clearing this site's browser storage and choosing again.

On portal.callmeie.ie (Document Ops customer portal):

  • Strictly-necessary session cookie for magic-link auth (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax).
  • Stripe cookies set during the checkout flow only.

On AI-First Websites we host for you: configurable per site. Default = self-hosted Umami (no cookies, IP-anonymised). If your site adds Stripe checkout or third-party widgets, those tools set their own cookies and you must disclose them in your own site's privacy notice.
