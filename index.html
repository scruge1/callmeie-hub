Callmeie Technologies is a one-person studio in Limerick. I started it because Irish SMBs — bookkeepers, dental clinics, vets, motor factors, freight forwarders, salons — keep paying for software that does the easy half of a job and leaves the hard half on someone's desk. The bookkeeper's desk. The receptionist's desk. The owner's desk after hours.

Every product here is shaped around the same idea: take the work that existing tools won't finish, finish it, and hand back something a team can actually use without re-checking. Receptionist answers the phone and writes Monday-morning summaries instead of you reading call logs. Document Ops reviews the uncertain rows OCR tools leave behind. AI-First Websites bundle both into the sites we build so your online presence is doing operating work, not just looking pretty.
